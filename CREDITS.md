# Sound credits

The recorded sounds in `audio/` come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org). Each is openly licensed: CC0 or public domain, or CC BY / CC BY-SA, which require this attribution.

- **Voices** are real speakers from the [Lingua Libre](https://lingualibre.org) project, saying the names of the heroes and their weapons in Hindi, Marathi, Sanskrit and English.
- **Format:** files were converted to MP3 by Wikimedia. Otherwise they are unmodified; the game plays short excerpts of the longer ones.
